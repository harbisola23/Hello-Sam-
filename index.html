How It All Started 💙

It all started at a tech event.

You were presenting for a website creation competition, and somehow, in the middle of everything happening that day, I walked up to you and said hi. From that simple hello, a whole friendship began.

You even helped me get a drink — I still can’t decide if it was a cocktail or a mocktail 😂 — and somehow, we ended up exchanging contacts.

And just like that… we became friends.

Two years later, it’s actually crazy to think about how much has happened since that day. We’ve had our moments, we’ve fallen out, found our way back, disappeared for a while, and then started talking again like absolutely nothing happened.

That’s probably one of my favourite things about our friendship.

We don’t have to talk every single day. We can go a while without speaking, but whenever we do talk again, there’s never that awkward “so why haven't you talked to me?” energy. 😂 It just feels normal. Like we simply pressed pause and continued where we left off.

And distance never really changed that.

From Abuja to Lagos, and then from Lagos to Imo State, somehow we’ve still managed to keep this friendship going.

We’ve had our pretty talks, our random conversations, our serious conversations, and those moments where we’ve just been there for each other emotionally.

So, Sam…
Thank you.

Thank you for being my friend through all the different versions of this friendship. Thank you for the laughs, the conversations, the advice, the random moments, and even the times we disappeared and somehow came back. 😂

Two years of friendship is something worth celebrating.

And honestly?

I’m really glad that random hello at that tech event turned into us. 💙

Here’s to almost two years, Sam.

And please… don't let this emotional episode get to your head. 😂
