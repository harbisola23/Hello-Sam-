How It All Started 💙

It all started at a tech event.

You were presenting for a website creation competition, and somehow, in the middle of everything happening that day, I walked up to you and said hi. From that simple hello, a whole friendship began.

You even helped me get a drink — I still can’t decide if it was a cocktail or a mocktail 😂 — and somehow, we ended up exchanging contacts.

And just like that… we became friends.

Two years later, it’s actually crazy to think about how much has happened since that day. We’ve had our moments, we’ve fallen out, found our way back, disappeared for a while, and then started talking again like absolutely nothing happened.

That’s probably one of my favourite things about our friendship.

We don’t have to talk every single day. We can go a while without speaking, but whenever we do talk again, there’s never that awkward “so why haven't you talked to me?” energy. 😂 It just feels normal. Like we simply pressed pause and continued where we left off.

And distance never really changed that.

From Abuja to Lagos, and then from Lagos to Imo State, somehow we’ve still managed to keep this friendship going.

We’ve had our pretty talks, our random conversations, our serious conversations, and those moments where we’ve just been there for each other emotionally.

So, Sam…
Thank you.

Thank you for being my friend through all the different versions of this friendship. Thank you for the laughs, the conversations, the advice, the random moments, and even the times we disappeared and somehow came back. 😂

Two years of friendship is something worth celebrating.

And honestly?

I’m really glad that random hello at that tech event turned into us. 💙

Here’s to almost two years, Sam.

And please… don't let this emotional episode get to your head. 😂

Important Legal Business 😂

Friendship Terms & Conditions

Effective from: That random day we met at the tech event.

Current status: Almost two years in. Still going strong.

Article 1 — The Friendship

By entering this friendship, both parties acknowledge that life will happen, schedules will get crazy, and responsibilities will sometimes take over.

However, being busy is not a valid reason to stop being intentional about the friendship.

Article 2 — The Disappearing Act

Temporary disappearance due to life being life may occur.

However, disappearing indefinitely without a valid reason is strictly prohibited. 😂

If either party gets caught up with life, an intentional check-in must eventually be made.

It doesn't have to be a long conversation.

A simple “Hey, I'm alive. Life is just doing the most. How are you?” is sufficient.

Because apparently, we believe in giving people grace and maintaining communication. 😂

Article 3 — The Catch-Up Clause

When life finally gives us breathing space, we shall pick up our conversations from wherever we left them.

No unnecessary guilt.

No “you don't care about me anymore” courtroom drama.

Just:

“So… where were we?”

And we continue.

Article 4 — Vulnerability

Both parties are permitted to be vulnerable without fear of being judged, misunderstood, or having their deepest moments used against them later.

Vulnerability shall be treated as trust, not weakness.

Article 5 — Emotional Support

Both parties shall endeavour to show up for each other — whether the situation requires serious advice, emotional support, laughter, or simply someone to listen.

Sometimes the solution may not be available. But presence still counts.

Article 6 — Intentionality

This friendship shall not run entirely on convenience.

Both parties agree to intentionally check in, intentionally make time, and intentionally maintain the connection — even when life gets busy.

Because good friendships don't survive on vibes alone. 😂

Article 7 — Distance

Abuja, Lagos, Imo State, or wherever life takes us next shall not determine the quality of this friendship.

Distance may change the frequency of seeing each other. It shall not determine the depth of the connection.

Article 8 — The Almost-Two-Year Clause

After almost two years, both parties acknowledge that this friendship has survived different cities, busy schedules, life changes, silence, conversations, laughter, serious moments, and everything in between.

At this point, we're clearly doing something right.

Article 9 — Termination

This friendship may not be terminated due to:

  • busy schedules
  • distance
  • delayed replies
  • temporary life chaos
  • misunderstandings
  • or the occasional “I'll reply later” that somehow becomes tomorrow 😂

Any serious issue shall first be discussed like responsible adults.

By continuing, you acknowledge that this friendship is built on trust, grace, vulnerability, communication, intentionality, and an unreasonable amount of memories.

There is no unsubscribe button.
You've been warned. 😂💙
